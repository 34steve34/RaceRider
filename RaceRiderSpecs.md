- 2D bike racing game

- Modeled after BIKE RACE (2012) from Top Free Games

- always played on mobile in landscape mode. Charging port on the right side

- rear wheel drive

- DART app

- most of the physics are custom

- Forge2D loaded and used for a few things, but not building drive physics for the bike

- bike composed of 3 points: 2 wheel and head

- independent suspension for rear and front wheels

- spokes of wheels are visually rotating realistically

- Brakes are exerted on both wheels at the same time.

- Applying the brakes with both wheels on the track will stop the bike faster than just with one wheel on the map when brakes applied.

- braking is done by touching the left side screen

- gas is done by touching the right side of the screen

- Physics are such that, if done with skill, a bike can climb a vertical wall. It is done by riding a very high wheelie with the head almost touching the ground behind the rear wheel.

- Bike crashes if the head hits the track

- Bike crashes if wheels hit the track too hard

- When gas is pressed, bike accelerates to its top speed (level track) in about 5 bike lengths.

- Brakes when applied to both wheels are about the same deceleration as the bike accelerates but reversed.

- Bike can go faster downhill.

- Bike goes slower when going against gravity.

- Bike will slow down when gas is released but will never stop completely on a level map.

- Bike will go backwards if no gas or brake and on an incline (even a small incline).

- Wheels exhibit a micro-magnetism to the track. The magnetism is exhibited on the top and bottom of the track.  It is used to improve game play.

- Wheel “micro-magnetism” is extremely weak and usually unnoticeable during normal riding.

- Bike is very stable and easy to ride a wheelie.

- always played in landscape mode with charging port on the right hand side. bike forward direction is right. to make front wheel go upwards the left side of the phone goes downwards.

- Leaning forward (nose down) never lifts up the rear wheel on level ground.

- Tilting applies a torque proportional (linear or not) to the angle of tilt of the phone.

- When riding a wheelie and the COG is above the wheel, no tilting (applied torque) is necessary because the bike is balanced in equilibrium. (this just let’s you know how tilting affects bike).

- when on rear wheel only, the rider can rapidly pull the front up or down (pulling down is very common when at the end of a upwards ramp in order to adjust the launch angle and minimize air-time.

- The only way to perform a ‘pro-lean’ at the level finish line is to lift up quickly on the front wheel then quickly slam it down hard and lean forward so bike goes up on the front wheel.  The head determines when the race is finished.  The COG/physics are such that you can never do that just by leaning forward (right side down).

- When going around the interior of a loop, the experienced player will keep the front wheel feather light on the track or just off the track to reach maximum speed.

- Eventually, tracks will be easy for players to design.  The track in the game is just for perfecting physics.

Assumptions:

- Wheels of bike have very high traction to track.  Enough to climb vertical wall

- The COG is placed so that gas will not tip the upwards into a wheelie. And also keeps front wheelies extremely difficult (see above).

- The forks are at angles (like a real motorcycle), not straight vertical from the ground.
